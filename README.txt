Samsung's newer appliances speak a local protocol on your own network, and this app speaks it back. Commands and readings go straight to the appliance's address on your LAN instead of out through SmartThings, so nothing about your home leaves it, and everything keeps working when the internet does not.

Air conditioners, induction cooktops, range hoods and refrigerators are supported, each verified against real hardware — a hood's fan and light, a fridge's temperature per compartment including a convertible one it tells you is currently cooling or freezing, an air conditioner's temperature, mode and comfort settings. All of it is available to a Flow: anything the appliance reports can be a condition, anything it accepts can be an action. Heat control is the one deliberate exception, read-only on cooking appliances, because an automation must never start a burner. Setup takes one client certificate, generated on your computer and pasted in once, and the app then finds your appliances by searching the network. Roughly 2022 and later models expose the local protocol; older ones keep no local port open and cannot be reached this way.
