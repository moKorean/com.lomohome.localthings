Samsung's newer appliances speak a local protocol on your own network, and this app speaks it back. Commands and readings go straight to the appliance's address on your LAN instead of out through SmartThings, so nothing about your home leaves it, and everything keeps working when the internet does not.

Air conditioners, induction cooktops, range hoods and refrigerators are supported, each verified against real hardware. An air conditioner exposes power, temperature, mode, fan and the comfort settings; a hood gives you its fan and its work lamp; a fridge reports and sets each compartment's temperature, including a convertible compartment currently being used as a fridge or a freezer; a cooktop reports what each burner is doing, and its heat controls are deliberately read-only, because an automation must never be able to start a burner. Setup takes one client certificate, generated on your computer and pasted in once, and the app then searches your network so you do not need to know any addresses. Roughly 2022 and later models expose the local protocol, while older ones keep no local port open and cannot be reached this way.
